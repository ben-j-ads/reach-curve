How it works

The model. Impressions = budget / CPM x 1,000. Effective impressions = signal weight x impressions. Reach = audience x (1 - e to the power of negative effective impressions over audience). Marginal return = signal weight x (1,000 / CPM) x e to the power of negative effective impressions over audience, in people per dollar. The knee is the spend where marginal return falls to 25% of its starting rate, which always lands at 75% of the audience.

The verdict. Before the knee, more spend still buys littles efficiently. Past the knee, each extra $1,000 buys less than a quarter of what the first $1,000 bought.

Toy model, not a measurement. It assumes impressions land randomly across the audience, so reach follows audience times one minus e to the power of negative effective impressions over audience. Signal weight scales effective impressions. Real curves come from delivery data. Use this to build intuition, not budgets.
